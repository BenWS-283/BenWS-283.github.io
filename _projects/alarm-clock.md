## Project Objectives
- Replace old, broken alarm clock with a new, custom built one without the old clock's problems
- Practice creating an entire project from scratch through every part of the design cycle

## Design Requirements
My previous alarm clock had a few design choices that made it annoying and inconvenient to set the alarm and clock times. Based on these problems, I came up with the following specific goals for this design:
- Ability to adjust minutes and hours separately, and in both directions
- Ability to switch to and stay in alarm or clock setting mode with the push of a button
- Easy access to edit code without taking it apart
- Ability to see at a glance whether the alarm is on (Added later in design cycle)
